# User research

Status: **not yet run** — no responses have been recorded yet. This file only contains real results once they exist. No invented people, quotes or numbers.

## Method
- **Survey (Google Form):** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScWrSStyRK5dNmzR1mL0Tefdtam-_ZutNAd2K4Ov_npCldLAA/viewform
  Target: at least 5 responses from club leads, mentors, members, or people who have handed a project over. Anonymous by default; quotes used only where the respondent answered "Yes" to "May we quote you anonymously?".
- **Short interviews (optional, 10–15 min):** same questions, in person or on a call. Respondents were told we use only fake demo data and not to share anything confidential.
- Raw responses are kept in the linked Google Sheet (not committed, to protect respondents).

## Questions asked
1. Role · 2. Team size · 3. How often people leave or join
4. How does a handover happen today? · 5. What was lost last time, and how did you find out? · 6. What kind of knowledge is lost most?
7. Would you trust a handover written by software (1–5)? · 8. What would you need to see first? · 9. Would you approve each statement before the new person sees it? · 10. What should never be shown?
11. (Optional) Anything confusing or missing in the demo? · 12. May we quote you anonymously?
